# Code Of Conduct

This project follows the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Be respectful and direct.
- Give constructive feedback.
- Assume good intent while staying accountable for impact.
- Keep discussions focused on the work.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, discriminatory language, threats, and publishing private information.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns to `smhassan223@gmail.com`. The maintainer may remove comments, close issues, block participants, or take other reasonable action to protect the project space.
